Service

SentinelOne is a Discord moderation bot and the associated website maintained by np15_.

Personal Data

Personal Data means information that can be used to identify or reasonably distinguish an individual, either directly or in combination with other information.

Collection and Usage of Data

SentinelOne collects and processes certain Discord server and member information solely to provide its moderation and server-management functionality.

The information collected or processed by the bot may include:

  • Server information: Server name and server ID for servers in which the bot is installed.
  • User information: User ID, username, and the date on which the user joined the server, where required for moderation or server-management functionality.
  • Message content: Messages may be logged when required for moderation, moderation review, auditing, or related server-management functionality.
  • Moderation information: Information relating to moderation actions performed through the bot, such as warnings, punishments, kicks, bans, mutes, or other configured moderation actions.

The data collected by SentinelOne is used only to provide, maintain, and improve the bot's moderation and server-management functionality. It is not used for advertising, user profiling, or the sale of personal information.

Message logs are collected solely for moderation-related purposes. They are not intended to be used to identify or profile users for purposes unrelated to moderation.

Retention of Data

Data is retained only for as long as it is reasonably required to provide the bot's moderation and server-management functionality.

Server and user-related information is retained while the bot remains associated with the relevant server or while the information is required for existing moderation records.

Message and moderation logs may be retained for moderation, auditing, and administrative purposes for as long as they are required by the bot or the server's moderation configuration.

If you believe that your personal information is being retained unnecessarily or that your privacy has been violated, you may contact us to request the removal of applicable data.

Protection of Data

SentinelOne takes reasonable measures to protect stored data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

User information and other stored data are stored in encrypted formats. Access to the database and stored information is restricted to the bot developer and operator.

No stored user information or message logs are intentionally shared with, sold to, or provided to third parties.

Third-Party Services

SentinelOne operates through Discord and therefore necessarily interacts with Discord's services and infrastructure. Discord may independently collect and process information in accordance with its own policies.

SentinelOne does not control the privacy practices, security, or data handling of Discord or any other third-party service. Users should review the applicable third-party privacy policies for information about how those services process data.

External Links

Our Service may contain links to other sites that are not operated by us. If you click a third-party link, you will be directed to that third party's site.

We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

Data Deletion Requests

Users may contact us through the Contact page to request deletion of personal information stored by SentinelOne.

Requests will be reviewed and applicable data will be removed where it is no longer required for the operation of the bot, legitimate moderation records, or other necessary purposes.

Updates to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to SentinelOne, its functionality, or its data-handling practices.

When material changes are made, the updated Privacy Policy will be made available on this page.
